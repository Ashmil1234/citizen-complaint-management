    Prototype architecture

    Built to complement official portals.

    Hack X is the triage and resolution intelligence layer between citizens, officers and authorised government systems.

    01

    Citizen intake

    Voice, Malayalam, English, WhatsApp-style text, location and evidence are converted into structured case data.

    02

    Human-readable triage

    Rules recommend the department, urgency and SLA while keeping confidence and reasoning visible to the reviewer.

    03

    Officer action

    Officers get a prioritised queue, missing-information prompts, handoff ownership and scheduled follow-up.

    04

    Measured resolution

    Management sees repeat complaints, SLA risk, citizen confirmation and root causes—not only closure counts.

    ✓

    Safety boundary

    The AI-style classifier is a transparent demo heuristic. It must not make final legal, disciplinary, eligibility, police-investigation or corruption findings. An authorised human must approve routing, replies and closure.
